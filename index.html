01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how cje, operated by cje — Consiliul Județean al Elevilor ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when users access the cje mobile application (the "App").

The App is designed to support student representation, communication, and access to county-level information. We aim to collect only the minimum personal information necessary for account creation and basic app functionality.

A dedicated Account and Data Deletion section is included below to explain how users, parents, legal guardians, or authorized representatives can request deletion of an account or associated personal data.

02 Data Controller

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, the data controller is cje — Consiliul Județean al Elevilor, Romania.

Privacy-related questions and requests can be submitted by email at app.consiliulelevilor@gmail.com or by phone at +40 784 953 870.

03 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to create and manage a user account in the App. The personal information collected is provided directly by the user during registration or account use.

We collect:

  • First name and last name — used to identify the user account.
  • Email address — used as the account login identifier and for account-related identification.
  • Selected county / regional affiliation — used to associate the user account with the appropriate county-level content and regional section of the App. This is provided manually by the user and is not collected from the device’s GPS, network location, or background location services.

We do not collect:

  • Device location collected from GPS, network location, or background location services
  • Contacts, phone book, call logs, or SMS data
  • Camera, microphone, photos, videos, or media files
  • Payment, billing, card, banking, or transaction information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics data, behavioural tracking data, profiling data, or heatmap data
  • Health data, biometric data, or government identification numbers

04 App Permissions

The App does not request or use permissions for device location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone calls, SMS, external storage, photos, videos, or payment/billing access.

If future versions of the App require additional permissions, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before or at the time such features are released.

05 How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected only for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain the user account.
  • To identify the user inside the App where account identification is necessary.
  • To associate the account with the correct county-level content and regional section of the App.
  • To respond to privacy, account, support, or deletion requests submitted by the user, parent, guardian, or authorized representative.
  • To maintain the basic security and integrity of the App.

The App does not use personal information for automated decision-making, scoring, behavioural profiling, or decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not use personal information for marketing emails, promotional messages, behavioural advertising, remarketing, retargeting, or selling or renting user data.

06 No Ads, Tracking, Analytics, or Payments

The App does not display advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

The App does not use analytics, behavioural tracking, heatmap, remarketing, retargeting, customer profiling, or advertising tools. This includes, but is not limited to, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AdMob, Mouseflow, Freshdesk, reCAPTCHA, and Google Places.

The App does not allow users to purchase products or services and does not collect payment, billing, card, banking, or transaction information.

07 Service Providers and Data Sharing

The App uses Supabase for authentication and database storage. Supabase processes personal information only as necessary to provide the App’s core functionality, such as account creation, authentication, secure database storage, security, and maintenance.

This service is not used for advertising, behavioural tracking, remarketing, or profiling.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising or commercial resale.

We may disclose personal information only if:

  • it is necessary to operate, secure, or maintain the App;
  • it is required by applicable law or a competent authority;
  • it is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the organization;
  • it is necessary to process an account deletion or data deletion request.

08 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as the user account remains active or as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

If an account is deleted or a deletion request is received, we will delete the associated personal data within 30 days, unless limited retention is required by applicable law.

Where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary to address a security, integrity, or legal issue, the retained information will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

The App is intended for use in a student representation context and may be used by students and minors. Users under the age of 13 may use the App only where permitted by applicable law and only with the required consent, supervision, or authorization.

We collect only the minimum information needed for account creation and basic use of the App: first name, last name, email address, and selected county / regional affiliation.

We do not collect children’s device location data, contacts, camera data, microphone data, photos, videos, payment data, advertising identifiers, behavioural tracking data, analytics data, health data, biometric data, or government identification numbers.

Users under the age of 13 may use the App only with consent and supervision from a parent or legal guardian, or with authorization from an educational institution or authorized educational representative where such authorization is legally valid and applicable.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected without appropriate consent or authorization where required, we will delete that information within a reasonable period.
